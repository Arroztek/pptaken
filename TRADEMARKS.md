# Trademark policy

PPTaken's code is open source and you are free to use it under its license. The name "PPTaken" and the PPTaken logo are not included in that license. This policy explains what you can do with them.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that something "works with PPTaken".
- Writing, speaking or making videos about the project.
- Distributing unmodified copies of PPTaken.
- Linking to the project.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Using the name or logo for a modified fork.
- Using the name or logo for a paid or hosted service.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests official endorsement by the PPTaken project.

To ask for permission, open a discussion on GitHub.

## Microsoft PowerPoint

PPTaken is not affiliated with Microsoft. Never use Microsoft PowerPoint's name, logo or iconography in a way that could suggest a connection to PPTaken.

## Notes

This policy is not legal advice. It is a friendly summary of our intentions, and it may be refined over time as the project evolves.
