# Governance

This document explains how PPTaken is run and how decisions are made.

## Current state

PPTaken currently has one maintainer who makes final decisions. We are honest about that: as the project grows, we expect to share responsibility with more people.

## Decision process

- Most decisions are discussed in issues and pull requests. Anyone can take part.
- We aim for lazy consensus: if nobody objects to a proposal after a reasonable discussion, it goes ahead.
- When there is no consensus, the maintainer makes the final decision and explains the reasoning in the discussion.
- Significant architectural decisions are recorded as Architecture Decision Records in [`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/README.md).

## Becoming a maintainer

We welcome people who want to take on more responsibility. Typically, a contributor is invited to become a maintainer after:

- sustained, good-quality contributions over time (code, reviews, documentation, support in discussions);
- a demonstrated understanding of the project's goals and values;
- an invitation from the existing maintainers.

Maintainers are listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md).

## Commitment to open source

PPTaken is open source, and we intend to keep it that way.

- The community edition (the code in this repository) will remain under an OSI-approved license: AGPL-3.0-or-later for the application, and Apache-2.0 for the format and player packages.
- A hosted cloud service is planned separately. Features that already exist in this repository will not be moved behind a paywall.
- Paid offerings will be about hosting and operating services for people, not about locking away features that the community already has.
